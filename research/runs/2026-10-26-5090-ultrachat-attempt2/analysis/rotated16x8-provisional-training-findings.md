# Rotated 16×8: provisional training findings

The training audit passes with no failed checks: 4,096 updates across 32 windows, comprising 3,264 soft and 832 hard updates. Every recorded parameter, gradient, update and Adam moment is finite and FP32; every hard-stage snapshot uses the actual last soft forward at step 102, sharpness 29.8828125. All expected gradient tensors are present, and snapshots were cleared after their windows. Evaluation, export and fresh reload are still in progress and are deliberately outside this review.

The current checkout source hashes match both the arm configuration and frozen launch gate. All 200 original-weight fingerprints in the initial manifest match the earlier sealed official-source export manifest. Initial raw factors and B match exact FP32 zero hashes; every A tensor has a nonzero FP32 hash. A changes on 127 of 128 steps in every window, while each factor family and B change on all 128. This is consistent with the zero initial B making the first A gradient zero; no gradient tensor is missing. A's accumulated update length is available, but its exact net displacement cannot be reconstructed from aggregate norms. Comparison with the independently initialized second arm remains pending.

The teacher bypass is bitwise exact before and after training. The research-state metadata and file length agree. The frozen writer explicitly verified all five saved parameter families as finite FP32 CPU tensors immediately before serialization; final optimizer-step statistics independently passed. This review did not deserialize or rehash the 1.29-GB checkpoint during GPU evaluation, so it does not claim an independent payload-integrity check.

All 32 windows improved local hidden-output MSE when evaluated with hard-quantized weights: the median after/before ratio is 0.11764, with a range of 0.03106–0.52120. These targets use the already quantized prefix. They differ from the Huber training objective and do not establish whole-model usefulness.

At the one representative projection recorded per window, final hard/source RMS has median 0.66398, zero occupancy 30.9586%, and correction/source Frobenius ratio 0.0036785. Effective/source RMS remains near one (median 1.0000092). These are 32 representative projections, not statistics over all 200 projections. The largest final absolute raw scale is 0.33900, implying a conservative scale multiplier bound of 0.83210–1.16790 relative to the effective centered mean absolute deviation plus 1e-6. Actual groupwise scale distributions and least-squares targets cannot be recovered from these JSON summaries. The saved final-layer hidden-state comparison has relative RMSE 1.09359 and cosine 0.41197; it is a drift measurement on one fixed record, not a quality decision.

Measured training took **1,303.8086 seconds**: cache capture 323.4307 seconds and optimization 926.9779 seconds, with remaining time in reconstruction evaluations, boundary audits, cleanup and overhead. Aggregated peak memory was **20.22055 GiB allocated / 20.296875 GiB reserved**, below the 28-GiB resource gate.

Detailed evidence is in `rotated16x8-training-audit.json` and `rotated16x8-provisional-training-findings.json`. No GPU work, checkpoint payload reads, runtime changes, or incomplete evaluation-artifact checks were performed by this audit.
